Privacy Policy

Racer #36 · The Compass Racing League
Last updated: 31 May 2026

Racer #36 ("we", "us", "the game") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using Racer #36 you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

When you create an account and play, we collect:

  • Account information — your racer name, email address, and password (passwords are securely managed by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us).
  • Gameplay data — your race scores, times, accuracy, chosen car, personalisation settings, friends, and head-to-head records.
  • Chat messages — public room and private messages you send within the game.
  • Usage data — basic technical information such as device type and browser, used to keep the game working well.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To run races, leaderboards, matchmaking, friends, and chat.
  • To save your progress and personalisation so it's there when you return.
  • To improve the game and fix problems.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, a service provided by Google. Firebase handles authentication and database storage on our behalf. You can read about Google's privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising

Racer #36 may display advertisements, including through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to Racer #36 and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Cookies

Racer #36 and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and serve relevant ads. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work properly without them.

Children's Privacy

Racer #36 is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Your Choices

  • You can update your racer name and personalisation settings at any time in the game.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.
  • You can opt out of personalised ads using the links in the Advertising section above.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers needed to run the game (such as Google Firebase) and advertising partners as described above. Your racer name, car, and race results are visible to other players as part of normal gameplay (leaderboards, chat, friends).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact us at the email address associated with Racer #36 support.
